Our Investment Strategy

Bozeman Capital Partners invests in short-duration residential real estate debt, alongside institutional private credit.

Our team has differentiated access to originate and finance the mortgage and working capital needs of seasoned real estate operators nationwide.

The Fund invests in loans that attach at a substantial discount to the market value of the real estate securing our loans, creating a high return profile with the structural protections of debt and a significant margin of safety.

Charles McKinney

Investment Structuring & Risk Management

Charles has a 22-year career as an entrepreneur, real estate investor, and financial services executive. He is the CEO and cofounder of Vontive and Certain Lending, fast-growing mortgage-technology and fintech lending businesses focused on real estate investors and backed by leading venture capital firms. Previously, Charles was senior vice president and head of analytics at Ten-X | Auction.com. From 2009 to 2014, Charles was one of the executives responsible for managing credit losses for Freddie Mac’s $1.4 trillion single-family mortgage portfolio, where he oversaw sales of more than 450,000 distressed properties to real estate investors.

Kunaal Kumar

Risk Analytics, Investor Reporting & Capital Raising

Kunaal covers risk analytics, investor reporting, capital raising, and portfolio management. He has an extensive background in computer science and quantitative finance, with experience developing algorithmic trading strategies for equities and derivatives, as well as working at a leading big tech firm. He began investing in real estate by building a rental portfolio in Seattle while working as a software engineer full-time, eventually transitioning to focus on capital raising and deploying capital into real estate credit strategies at scale.
